Privacy Policy
Effective 08/18/26

Commons is built to collect as little information about you as possible. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what happens to it when you delete your account.
At a glance:

  • Minimal data, by design — Just your email, username, photo, and bio — nothing more.
  • No targeted ads — Ads are random placements, never based on your activity or profile.
  • Delete anytime — Deleting your account removes your data immediately, with narrow exceptions.

This box is a plain-language summary only. The full policy below is what governs how Commons handles your information.

1. Overview This Privacy Policy explains how Propicks LLC ("Propicks," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use Commons (the "Service"), our anti-algorithm social media app, available now on the Apple App Store with an Android release coming soon. Commons is built to collect the minimum amount of information necessary to operate the app — no more.

By using Commons, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you have questions, contact us using the details in Section 16.

2. Information we collect When you create a Commons account, we collect the email address you provide, the username you choose, your profile photo, and your profile description. We do not collect a government ID, phone number, precise location, or your device contacts, and we do not require any additional personal information to use the app.

3. Data stored with Firebase Your account email, password, and any photos you upload — including your profile photo and photos posted to Commons — are stored using Google Firebase, a third-party cloud infrastructure provider. Firebase stores this data on our behalf and under our direction; Propicks does not sell or share this data with Firebase for Firebase's own purposes.

Passwords are stored using Firebase's built-in secure authentication system. Propicks does not have access to your plaintext password.

4. How we use information We use the information we collect to create and maintain your account, display your username, profile photo, and description to other users as intended by the app, operate the chronological feed, respond to support requests, and enforce our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, including reviewing reports of prohibited content.

We do not use your information to build advertising profiles, and Commons does not run an engagement-optimization algorithm that relies on behavioral data to rank or recommend content to you.

5. Content moderation & automated review To help keep Commons safe, content posted to the app is automatically screened using OpenAI's moderation endpoint, a third-party content classification service. This automated screening flags content that may violate our Community Guidelines or applicable law for further review. Content sent for this screening is used only to evaluate it against moderation categories.

6. IP address collection In most cases, we do not collect or store your IP address. However, if automated moderation or a user report identifies content that may be illegal, we briefly retrieve the IP address associated with the account that posted the content and log it together with a record of the specific infraction identified.

This limited IP logging exists solely to support safety and legal-compliance investigations related to potentially illegal content, and is not performed for ordinary content or routine account activity.

7. Advertising Commons displays advertising through Google AdMob. Ads on Commons are not targeted to you based on your activity, profile, or personal information — they function more like static, rotating placements rather than behaviorally targeted advertising. We do not share your account information, content, or activity with AdMob, other advertisers, or any third-party advertising network for targeting purposes.

AdMob may independently collect limited technical or device-level data under its own privacy practices in order to serve and measure ads; you can review Google's policies for more detail on that processing.

8. Sharing of information We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing or advertising purposes. We share information only as described in this policy — with Firebase (Section 3) and OpenAI's moderation endpoint (Section 5) as service providers processing data on our behalf — or where required to comply with law, protect the safety of users, or investigate a potential violation of our Terms of Service.

9. Retention & account deletion When you delete your Commons account, we immediately delete your account data — including your email, username, profile photo, description, and associated content — from our databases and from Firebase, except as described below.

If your account is under active review for a suspected violation of our Community Guidelines, or for content that may be illegal, we retain the relevant account data, content, and any logged IP address until that review is complete, even if you delete your account during that time. This limited retention exists to support safety review and, where applicable, legal compliance.

10. Reporting & review process Commons allows users to report posts that may violate our Community Guidelines or applicable law, including reports for suspected misinformation or for AI-generated content, which is not permitted on Commons. Reported posts are reviewed within 48 hours.

Posts reported for potentially violating the law are automatically hidden from other users while under review, since such reports may involve illegal or distressing content that has not yet been reviewed by our team.

11. Children & minors Commons is not intended for children under 13, and use by minors under 18 requires the consent of a parent or guardian, as described in our Terms of Service. We do not currently use independent age-verification technology and rely on the age provided at signup. We've designed Commons' features, moderation practices, and chronological feed with the safety of younger users in mind.

If you believe a child has created an account or provided us information in violation of this policy, contact us using the details in Section 16.

12. Data security We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect your information, including relying on Firebase's secure infrastructure for authentication and storage. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Your choices & rights You can review and update your username, profile photo, and description at any time within the app. You can delete your account at any time, which deletes your data as described in Section 9. If you have questions about the information we hold about you, or would like to make a request regarding your data, contact us using the details in Section 16.

14. International users Commons is operated from the United States, and information we collect is stored and processed in the United States, including through Firebase's US-based infrastructure. If you use Commons from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

15. Changes to this policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will provide notice, such as through the Service or by email, before the changes take effect. Continued use of Commons after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
