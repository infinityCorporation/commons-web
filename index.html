why we built this

We left a few things off the guest list.

Every major social media quietly picked its priorities for you. We picked ours the opposite way by deciding what to leave out.

  • An algorithm deciding who sees your photosEveryone in your circle sees everything, in the order it happened.
  • Ads between every third postNo feed ads. Commons isn't funded by your attention.
  • Strangers weighing in on your family tripOnly the people you invite can see or comment. Ever.
  • Follower counts and public likesNo public metrics. Nothing here is a popularity contest.

how it works

Three steps. That's the whole app.

  1. 01

    Build your circle

    Invite the people you actually want to share with — family, close friends, a group chat's worth of people. No public profile, no strangers finding you.

  2. 02

    Share in order, not by algorithm

    Photos show up the way you'd expect: newest first, from the people you follow. Nothing is ranked, boosted, or hidden to keep you scrolling.

  3. 03

    React with people you know

    Comments and reactions stay inside your circle — never on a public timeline, never searchable, never screenshotted into an argument.

what's built in

Simple to use. Simple to trust.

Private circles

Every photo is shared with a specific group you choose — not the whole app.

Zero ads, ever

No sponsored posts, no data brokers. If we ever charge, you'll know exactly what for.

No algorithmic feed

Chronological, always. What you see is what was actually shared, in the order it happened.

No public metrics

No follower counts, no like totals on display. Nothing here to compete over.

Easy to manage

Leave a circle, mute a thread, or delete a photo in two taps. Nothing lingers by design.

Built for families

Multi-generational by design — as easy for a grandparent as it is for a teenager.

questions

A few things people ask.

When does Commons launch?

We're inviting people off the waitlist in small batches as the app is ready. Join the list above and we'll email you when it's your turn.

Which platforms will it support?

Commons is launching on iOS first with an Android app planned for the future.

Will it always be free?

Commons will always have a free way to share with your circle. If we ever add a paid tier, it'll be for optional extras — never for ad-free basics, since there are no ads to begin with.

How is this different from a private Instagram account?

A private account still lives inside an algorithmic, ad-funded feed. Commons is built from the ground up without either — chronological, ad-free, and only visible to the circles you choose.
